# TechNova Pvt Ltd — HR Policy Handbook

## Leave Policy
Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per year, credited monthly (1 per month). Must be applied at least 1 day in advance via the HR portal.
Sick Leave (SL): 8 days per year. Medical certificate required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
Earned Leave (EL): 15 days per year, accrued quarterly. Can be carried forward up to 30 days; unused leave beyond that lapses at year-end.
Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per the Maternity Benefit Act, applicable to female employees with at least 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months.
Paternity Leave: 15 days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.
Leave Without Pay (LWP): Requires manager and HR approval, applicable only after all other leave balances are exhausted.

## Working Hours
Standard working hours: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Core hours (mandatory presence): 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Flexible start time allowed between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM, provided 9 hours (including 1-hour lunch break) are completed.

## Probation & Confirmation
New employees undergo a 6-month probation period. Confirmation is based on a performance review conducted in the 5th month by the reporting manager. Probation may be extended by up to 3 months in case of performance concerns, with written notice.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must maintain confidentiality of company and client data at all times, including after separation from the company. Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited and can be reported confidentially to hr-grievance@technova.com. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately.

## Reimbursements
Travel reimbursement: Submit bills within 30 days of travel via the Expense Portal. Approval required from reporting manager. Internet/mobile reimbursement for remote employees: Up to ₹1,500/month. Reimbursements are processed within 15 working days of approval.

## Resignation & Notice Period
Standard notice period: 60 days for employees with more than 1 year of service; 30 days for employees with less than 1 year. Buyout of notice period is possible with manager and HR approval. Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
