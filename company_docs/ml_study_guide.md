# TechNova Pvt Ltd — New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day 1
HR sends a welcome email with employee ID, company email credentials, and a checklist 3 days before joining. Complete bank details and PF nomination form via the HR Portal before Day 1.

## Day 1 Checklist
Report to reception at 9:30 AM with original ID proof and educational certificates. Collect laptop and access card from the IT desk (Room 204). Attend HR induction session (10:30 AM, Conference Room B). Meet your reporting manager and assigned buddy. Get added to relevant Slack channels and Google Workspace groups.

## First Week
Complete mandatory compliance training (POSH, Information Security, Code of Conduct) on the Learning Portal — deadline is 5 working days from joining. Set up your development environment with help from your buddy or team lead. Schedule 1:1s with your manager and key team members.

## First 30 Days
Manager sets 30-60-90 day goals during your first week. A probation check-in happens informally at the 30-day mark. HR conducts a "New Hire Pulse Survey" at 30 days.

## Key Contacts
HR: hr-support@technova.com. IT: helpdesk portal or ext. 4040. Payroll: payroll@technova.com. Facilities: facilities@technova.com.

## Benefits Overview
Group health insurance covering employee + family (spouse, up to 2 children), active from Day 1. Meal cards worth ₹2,200/month, credited on the 1st of every month. Annual learning allowance of ₹15,000 for certifications, reimbursable with manager approval.
